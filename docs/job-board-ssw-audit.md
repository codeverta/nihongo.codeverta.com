## Implementation guidance

The immediate low-risk candidate is TOKUTEI JOB MATCHING because the supplied public page is explicitly SSW-specific and contains visible featured-job links. Agri-Navi is a plausible agriculture source because public job-detail links are present in its homepage HTML, though each posting must be checked for visa suitability. The other observed pages are mostly portals, search forms, or general job sites; the supplied URL alone did not demonstrate a stable public listing feed.

Treat source-specific fields as nullable observations. In particular, use `unknown`/null for salary, visa or residence-status eligibility, Japanese level, and SSW sector unless the individual posting explicitly states them. A job in a matching industry, a candidate-profile form asking for visa type, a salary filter, and generic service copy do not establish those job facts. Keep the original posting URL, observation timestamp, and raw source text for every extracted value.

This was a first-page feasibility check, not a review of every site's terms of service or a production-scale stability test. A robots 404 is recorded as “no robots file found” evidence only, not as an affirmative license. Before routine ingestion, review applicable site terms and check any candidate detail route once under the same public-access constraints.
