# SSW curriculum sources: food, forestry, wood, and recycling

Research checked on **2026-10-22**. These links are official Japanese government, designated test-body, or industry-body pages. The curricula use original Indonesian explanations and workplace scenarios based on the public scope below. They do not reproduce official test questions or protected study text.

## Food manufacturing

The target work covers food and beverage manufacturing. The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (MAFF) identifies OTAFF as the designated SSW skill-test body and provides the sector’s skills-test and Japanese-language-test requirements. Use OTAFF’s current guide for the current test schedule, registration and format; these details can change. The curriculum therefore teaches hygiene, HACCP-based process controls, product specifications, allergen cross-contact prevention, temperature records, packaging and escalation without claiming universal critical limits. Workers must follow the facility’s approved plan and product instructions. MHLW describes HACCP-based hygiene management as the applicable framework, while the Consumer Affairs Agency publishes current allergen-label rules; check current product specifications rather than relying on a memorized allergen list.

- [MAFF: Specified Skilled Worker in food manufacturing](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/sanki/soumu/tokuteiginou.html)
- [OTAFF: SSW food-manufacturing test guide](https://otaff.or.jp/tokutei/insyoku-1/)
- [MHLW: HACCP-based hygiene management](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/shokuhin/haccp/01_00020.html)
- [Consumer Affairs Agency: food-allergy labeling](https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/food_labeling/food_sanitation/allergy/)

## Food service

MAFF identifies OTAFF as the test administrator and the Japan Food Service Association (JFNet) as the source of official study materials. OTAFF’s SSW(i) guide describes a theory and practical test; the actual current guide controls the delivery details. OTAFF announced that SSW(i) food-service test reservations stopped on 2026-03-27 at MAFF’s direction. The Immigration Services Agency’s SSW visa page also marks the field as not accepting new SSW(i) applications at the date checked. OTAFF’s notice says the SSW(ii) food-service test and food-manufacturing tests remain available. This is an intake and exam-status caveat, not a reason to withhold educational material: the lessons remain useful for workplace Japanese and hospitality learning. Learners should recheck the official status before applying or booking.

## Forestry

The Forestry Agency lists forestry as an SSW field, publishes the sector-specific guide and identifies the Forestry Skills Improvement Center as the test body. Public test guidance covers forestry work such as silviculture and log production and describes a skills assessment. The lessons focus on safe communication, work planning, PPE, chainsaw and machine exclusion zones, tree-felling hazards, weather, environmental care and reporting. They do not authorize a learner to operate machinery: required training, workplace procedures, supervision and local risk controls still apply. MHLW’s chain-saw safety material supports the safety emphasis.

- [Forestry Agency: SSW information for forestry](https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/j/routai/gaikoku.html)
- [Forestry Agency: forestry skill-test overview](https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/j/routai/attach/pdf/gaikoku-26.pdf)
- [Forestry Skills Improvement Center: official test information](https://ringyou-gino.org/)
- [Forestry Agency: learning material for foreign forestry workers](https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/j/routai/attach/pdf/gaikoku-28.pdf)

## Wood industry

The Forestry Agency defines the sector and links the official test, implementation rules and study materials. Its specified work includes designated wood-processing activities such as lumber, veneer, chips, plywood/LVL, glulam and related construction-wood products; this does not mean every woodworking, furniture or installation job is covered. Zenmokuren is the test body. Its current notice says the test moves to CBT from fiscal year 2026, so learners must use the latest official test page for registration and delivery details. The lessons cover receiving and identifying material, machine guarding, cutting/pressing safety, dimensions, moisture and surface checks, traceability and defect reporting.

- [Forestry Agency: SSW in the wood industry](https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/j/mokusan/seisankakou/foreigner.html)
- [Forestry Agency: wood-industry target work and scope Q&A](https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/j/mokusan/seisankakou/attach/pdf/foreigner-21.pdf)
- [Zenmokuren: official foreign-worker skill-test information](https://zenmokuren.jp/foreigner/)
- [Prometric Japan: SSW wood-industry test information](https://www.prometric-jp.com/ssw/test_list/archives/26)

## Resource recycling

The Environment Ministry’s sector page defines the field around waste intermediate-treatment work and lists receiving, sorting, treatment and shipment-related tasks. Its current page says the skill-test details will be published when determined. The Ministry’s February 2026 overview records approval of the field in January and a planned intake start in 2027. By the access date, the Ministry also linked an official field-specific support site with operating information published through October 2026. However, the Environment Ministry’s current SSW page still says the specific skill-test details will be announced when decided. Consequently this curriculum teaches general sorting discipline, safe handling, contamination control, equipment-zone awareness, load identity and traceability; it is not an official exam-preparation syllabus, and it does not claim a test body, exam format or pass mark. Confirm the newest Environment Ministry and Immigration Services Agency notices before making a status or test decision.

## Scope and limitations

The material is an introductory A1–A2 Indonesian-language learning aid, not legal advice, employer-specific SOP, occupational certification, food-safety plan, or permission to perform hazardous work. It uses safe response patterns—pause, isolate when appropriate, keep records, and ask a supervisor—because site procedures and permitted duties vary. It avoids fixed temperature limits, chemicals, machine settings and other values that must come from a workplace plan or product instructions. Official test formats, schedules, scope and entry status can change; the linked official pages control. Resource recycling is especially provisional because test administration and assessment details had not been announced at the access date.
