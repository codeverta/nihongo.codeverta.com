## Full description

Learn Japanese step by step with Nihongo Master. Study JLPT vocabulary, kanji, and grammar from N5 through N1, practise with original questions, and keep your progress in one place.

**Choose how you learn**

• Learn Japanese with explanations in English or Indonesian.\
• Keep Japanese words, kanji, and example sentences in their original form.\
• Review vocabulary with pictures, readings, and example sentences.\
• Learn grammar patterns with clear explanations and practice.\
• Study kanji with readings, stroke counts, memory hints, and writing practice.\
• Read illustrated stories with optional kana and translation help.

**Prepare with practice**

• Take the JLPT N5 diagnostic to find a starting point.\
• Practise with JLPT-style N5, N4, and N3 tests.\
• Review available JFT-Basic and NAT practice exams.\
• See results and explanations after completing a test.

**More ways to use Japanese**

• Search a Japanese dictionary, even without signing in.\
• Read Japanese news from publishers in its original form.\
• Explore Japanese manga with a right-to-left reader.\
• Practise with topic-based flashcards.\
• Explore available Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) learning materials and job listings.

N5 and N4 learning materials and N5 tests are available for free. A one-time Lifetime Access purchase unlocks the premium materials and practice tests shown in the app. The store displays the price and currency for your billing region before you confirm. Lifetime Access is not a recurring subscription.

Nihongo Master creates its own practice materials. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the JLPT, JFT-Basic, NAT, or other exam providers. Practice tests do not provide official exam scores or guarantee a result.
