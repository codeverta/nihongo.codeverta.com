This page explains how to request deletion of a Nihongo Master account operated by Codeverta, including when the app is no longer installed. You do not need to sign in to this page.

In the app

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile and choose Delete account.
  3. Read the explanation and confirm permanent deletion.

By email, without the app

From the email address on your account, write to {{.Email}} with the subject Nihongo Master account deletion request. State the account email and whether you are requesting full account deletion or a review of particular data or backups. Our team may ask you to verify ownership before processing. Do not send passwords, login tokens, full purchase tokens, or card numbers.

Email a deletion request

If your device has no email app, open your email service and send the request manually to the address above. Email requests are handled manually; opening this page does not automatically delete anything.

Data deleted and retained

Deletion removes the account and profile, progress and achievements, account billing token, and related sessions and attempts from active systems. Minimum transaction evidence is detached from the former account ID and retained for purchase restoration, reconciliation, and replay prevention for as long as the associated entitlement or purpose applies. The shared dictionary cache remains. Backups and support correspondence follow the privacy retention policy; backups currently have no automatic expiry schedule.

Clear the app's data on your device to remove local copies. Account deletion does not automatically request a refund or cancel obligations with the store. Account data deletion is permanent; creating a new account will not restore deleted progress.
