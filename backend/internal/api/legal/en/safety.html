Public endpoints use HTTPS. Uploaded profile photos are stored at a public URL that anyone with the URL may be able to open. This version uses Firebase Analytics for feature-usage analytics (Android; can be turned off in Settings) and Google AdMob for ads shown to free users (Android), and does not request location, contacts, or microphone permissions. The camera is accessed only if you choose Take photo; gallery images are selected through the system picker. Network, sign-in, store, dictionary, and content providers process data as needed for the features you use. This statement does not mean the app collects no data.

Account deletion is available in the app and by email request. Purchase evidence and backup retention are described in the privacy policy. Google Play Data safety answers must cover the complete build and SDKs you distribute and should be updated if their behavior changes.
