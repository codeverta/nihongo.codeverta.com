This policy covers Nihongo Master purchases through Google Play and, if available, the App Store.

Lifetime access and verification

A lifetime product is a one-time purchase for the premium features included in that product when you buy it. It is not a recurring subscription and does not promise that the app or every feature will be available forever. The store displays the applicable price, currency, taxes, and product details before you confirm. A pending, cancelled, or unsuccessful purchase does not grant access; access is activated after the transaction evidence is verified.

Google Play refund requests

Eligibility, request timing, payment method, location, and the refund decision are governed by Google Play's policies and applicable consumer rights. We cannot guarantee that every request will be approved. Use official Google Play refund help, or contact {{.Email}} if there is a problem with your transaction. Include your account email, Google Play order ID (GPA...), purchase date, and a description of the problem. Do not send card details or passwords.

App Store

Apple purchases are handled through Apple Report a Problem and Apple refund help and policy.

Restore and changes to access

After reinstalling or moving to a supported device, use Restore purchases in the app with the store account that made the purchase. A refunded or revoked purchase may disable premium access after store verification. Deleting your app account is not a refund request; minimum transaction evidence is retained for restoration and replay prevention. Contact us if you paid but access has not been activated. These terms do not limit mandatory rights under applicable consumer law.
