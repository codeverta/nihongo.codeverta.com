Frequently asked questions.

Is the app really free?

Yes. N5 and N4 materials, all N5 tests, the dictionary, and news are available for free. Lifetime Access is required for N3 through N1 materials and other exam practice tests.

Is Lifetime Access a subscription?

No. You pay once through Google Play, and access is linked to your Nihongo Master account. There are no automatic renewals.

Will I lose access if I get a new phone?

Sign in with the same account and use Restore purchases. Your learning progress is also saved to your account, not just your phone.

Are these official JLPT questions?

No. Nihongo Master creates original practice questions with formats and instructions similar to an exam. They are practice materials, not leaked questions or official exam results.

How do I sign in?

Use a Google account or email address. Guests can still use the dictionary and news without signing in.

How can I delete my account and data?

Use the Profile menu in the app, or follow the steps on our account and data deletion page. For other questions, email contact@codeverta.com.
